# Bogeumi stage wardrobe artwork

Mode: built-in image_gen; transparent reference-based edits.

Four 3×3 fitted character sheets and four matching transparent accessory sheets. Runtime uses stage-specific base artwork and calibrated accessory overlays. Original source sheets remain available for visual reference.

## Stage 1 character sheet

Create a production transparent sprite atlas for this exact cute 3D clay house mascot. Stage 1 seedling home: use only top-left character from reference 1, little rounded cream house with blue roof, small paws and feet, open dark eyes, pink cheeks, cheerful mouth. No book, ID badge, jacket or key.
Square canvas, strict 3 by 3 equal cells, no text or cell borders. All nine figures are EXACT clones with same pose, face, anatomy, scale, position and lighting, centered in each cell, filling 78 percent of cell width and height. Feet baseline at 88 percent cell height. Reference 2 supplies accessory design only. Make equipment convincingly worn, sculpted in same soft clay material, straps wrapping behind body, correct depth occlusion and contact shadows. No floating objects.
Row-major cells:
1 naked base (original stage clothing retained).
2 round BLUE glasses on the eyes, bridge across nose and temples wrapping sides.
3 pink ribbon anchored to upper right roof slope.
4 LAVENDER over-ear headphones with cream ear cushions and tiny gold heart, band wraps roof top and cushions against sides.
5 coral knitted scarf snugly around lower face/body neck with tail resting along right body.
6 small gold star brooch attached on body's upper right.
7 mint beret sitting on left roof slope.
8 small blue satchel with cream flap/gold button, strap crosses body, bag rests at right hip behind paw.
9 small gold house charm hanging from a short loop attached near left paw.
Keep body pixels identical between variants wherever equipment doesn't obscure them. Open eyes centered at x43% and57%, y62% of every cell; roof at y20-48%, paws y75%, feet y84%. Crisp transparent alpha, no background, no shadow outside each figure. All four stage sheets must use this coordinate registration.
